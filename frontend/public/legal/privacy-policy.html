Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 28, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to Seamount.io ("Seamount," "we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy outlines how Seamount Technologies Ltd. collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use our website, mobile application, and services (collectively, the "Services"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency in how we handle your data, in compliance with international standards and the specific regulations of the jurisdictions we operate in, including Nigeria.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, information collected automatically, and information from third-party sources.

2.1. Information You Provide

  • Personal Identification Information: Name, date of birth, nationality, gender, government-issued identification numbers (BVN, NIN, Passport number), and photographs.
  • Contact Information: Email address, phone number, and residential address.
  • Financial Information: Bank account details, mobile money information, and transaction history.

2.2. Information Collected Automatically

  • Transactional Information: Public blockchain data related to your transactions, including wallet addresses, transaction amounts, and timestamps on the Algorand Mainnet.
  • Device and Connection Information: IP address, device type, operating system, browser type, and service provider information.
  • Usage Information: Information about how you interact with our Services, including access times, pages viewed, and links clicked.

2.3. Information from Third Parties

We collect information from our third-party service providers as a necessary part of our regulated financial services:

  • KYC/AML Providers (ComplyCube, Regfyl): We receive identity verification results, risk assessments, and sanctions screening reports to comply with Anti-Money Laundering (AML) and Counter-Terrorist Financing (CTF) regulations.
  • Payment Providers (Flutterwave, Paystack): We receive confirmation of fiat deposits and withdrawals, including transaction IDs and status, to facilitate on-ramping and off-ramping.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • To Provide and Maintain our Services: To create and manage your account, process transactions, and provide customer support.
  • For Compliance and Security: To verify your identity, comply with legal obligations (such as the ISA Act in Nigeria, MiCA in the EU, and FATF Travel Rule), prevent fraud, and protect the security of our platform.
  • To Improve Our Services: To analyze usage trends and improve the functionality and user experience of our platform. This includes using aggregated and anonymized data to inform the development of future products, such as cybersecurity services.
  • To Communicate With You: To send you transactional confirmations, technical notices, security alerts, and support messages.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with the following parties for the purposes described in this policy:

  • With Your Consent: We may share your information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.
  • With Service Providers: We share information with our trusted partners who perform services on our behalf, such as KYC/AML verification, payment processing, and cloud hosting. These partners are contractually obligated to protect your data and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.
  • For Legal Reasons: We may disclose your information if required by law, subpoena, or other legal process, or if we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety, or investigate fraud.
  • Blockchain Data: Please be aware that transactions conducted on the Algorand blockchain are public and immutable. Your wallet address and transaction details will be permanently recorded and publicly visible.

5. Data Retention

We retain your personal information for as long as necessary to provide our Services to you, to comply with our legal obligations (typically 5-7 years for financial records), to resolve disputes, and to enforce our agreements.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, or delete your data. To exercise these rights, please contact us at support@seamount.io.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at legal@seamount.io.
