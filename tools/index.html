Understanding Your Metering Options

There are three main ways to get a prepaid meter in EKEDC's network. We help you navigate all options to find the best fit for your situation.

FREE

DISREP

Nigeria Distribution Sector Recovery Program sponsored by The World Bank.

  • Completely free meter & installation
  • World Bank funded program
  • Specific eligibility criteria apply
Limited availability, subject to program guidelines
FREE

MAF

Meter Acquisition Fund funded by the Federal Government.

  • Free meter, supply cable & installation
  • For Band A & B customers
Limited availability, subject to program guidelines
PAID - FAST TRACK

MAP

Meter Asset Provider - Customer-funded option for faster installation.

  • Faster installation timeline
  • Pay for meter & installation
  • Available to all customers
Get installed in days, not months

Not sure which scheme is right for you? Our experts will assess your eligibility and recommend the best option during the application process.
