Your Metering Options

Three main ways to get a prepaid meter. We help you find the best fit.

FREE

DISREP

World Bank-funded distribution recovery program.

  • Completely free meter & installation
  • World Bank funded
  • Specific eligibility criteria
Limited availability
FREE

MAF

Meter Acquisition Fund by Federal Government.

  • Free meter, cable & installation
  • For Band A & B customers
Limited availability
PAID - FAST

MAP

Customer-funded for faster installation.

  • Faster installation timeline
  • Pay for meter & installation
  • Available to all customers
